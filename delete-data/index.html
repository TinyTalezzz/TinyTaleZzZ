Delete your TinyTaleZzZ data

TinyTaleZzZ: Bedtime Stories, by KSEV3N Labs

In short

TinyTaleZzZ has no accounts of its own and no server that keeps your stories. Your stories live on your phone, and in your own Google Drive only if you turned on sync. You can delete all of it yourself, at any time, with the steps below. The only thing we ever receive is a story you choose to report, and we delete those on request.

1. Stories on your phone

  1. To delete one story: in the Library, press and hold the story, then tap Delete.
  2. To delete every story: open Settings and, under Storage, tap Clear story history.
  3. To delete everything the app keeps on your phone (stories, settings and your OpenRouter key): uninstall the app.

These are deleted straight away. If sync is on, a deleted story is also deleted on your other synced devices.

2. Stories synced to your Google Drive

If you turned on Sync across devices, a copy of your stories and some settings is kept in a private, hidden TinyTaleZzZ folder in your own Google Drive. To delete it:

  1. In the app, open Settings, then under Sync across devices tap Delete the synced copy from Google Drive. This deletes the folder's contents and turns sync off on that phone. Or:
  2. In Google Drive on the web, open Settings → Manage apps, find TinyTaleZzZ, and choose Delete hidden app data.
  3. To also remove the app's access to your Google Account, visit myaccount.google.com/connections and remove TinyTaleZzZ.

This copy is stored by Google under your account. We never receive it and cannot see it.

3. Stories you reported to us

When you use Report a problem with this story, we receive that story's text and the reason you chose, with the characters' names replaced by placeholders. To have a report deleted, email tinytalezzzapp@gmail.com with the story's title and the date you sent it. We delete it within 30 days. Every report is deleted automatically after at most two years anyway.

What other companies keep

  • OpenRouter and the AI provider that wrote a story received your story choices through your own OpenRouter account. Their own policies say how long they keep them; you can manage or delete your OpenRouter account at openrouter.ai.
  • Google AdMob receives limited technical information to show non-personalized ads, as described in our privacy policy, and keeps it under Google's own policies.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy. Questions: tinytalezzzapp@gmail.com.
