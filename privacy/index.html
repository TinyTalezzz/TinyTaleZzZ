TinyTaleZzZ Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TinyTaleZzZ mobile app (the “App”) and this website handle information. “TinyTaleZzZ”, “we”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of the App. “You” means the parent, legal guardian or other adult using the App. Please read it together with our Terms of Use.

The short version

  • Your stories stay on your phone. There are no TinyTaleZzZ accounts, and we do not run any server that receives or stores your stories. If you choose to turn on Sync across devices, a copy is kept in a private folder in your own Google Drive, which we cannot see.
  • To write a story, the App sends the choices you make (such as the main characters’ first names) to OpenRouter, an AI service, using your own OpenRouter account. We do not receive these requests.
  • The App shows short video ads from Google AdMob. Every user is treated as a child for advertising: the ads are not personalized, are limited to content suitable for all ages, and the App does not use your phone’s advertising ID.
  • If you choose to report a story, we receive that story’s text with the characters’ names removed.
  • We do not sell or rent personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we have no analytics or tracking of our own.

1. Who we are

TinyTaleZzZ is a small, independent app developer. For the purposes of data protection law, TinyTaleZzZ is responsible for the limited information described here that we receive ourselves (reports you choose to send, and emails you write to us). You can reach us at tinytalezzzapp@gmail.com.

2. Children and families

The App is designed to be used by a parent, legal guardian or other responsible adult, together with a child, to create and read bedtime stories. It is not designed for children to use on their own, and the person setting up and operating the App should be an adult.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App has no accounts of its own, sign-ups, chat, social features, profiles, location tracking, camera, microphone or contact-list access. The optional sync feature uses a parent’s own Google account and is for adults only (see section 7).
  • The only information about a child that the App handles is what the adult chooses to type into a story: usually a first name or nickname, and descriptions such as personality traits. A first name or nickname is all a story needs. Please do not enter a child’s full name, surname, address, school, birthday, photo or other identifying details.
  • Story choices are sent to OpenRouter to write the story (see section 4). We do not receive them. When a story is reported to us, the characters’ names are removed first (see section 6).
  • Advertising is configured for children: ads are not personalized, are restricted to content rated suitable for all ages, and the App does not use the advertising ID (see section 5).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have received personal information about your child, please contact us at tinytalezzzapp@gmail.com and we will delete it.

3. Information that stays on your phone

The following is stored only in the App’s private storage on your phone. We cannot see or access it.

  • Your stories: the text of each story, the choices used to make it (theme, main characters, traits, listener age group, companion, loved one, place, quest, length), a short summary, favorites, and the date it was made.
  • Your settings: text size, story length, narrator voice and reading speed, interactive-story preference, bedtime-reminder time, and a count of how many stories have been made (used for the free-story allowance).
  • Your OpenRouter key, if you connect one. It is kept in your phone’s secure, encrypted storage and is only ever sent to OpenRouter. We never receive it.
  • Temporary files, such as a storybook PDF or a backup file while it is being shared, are kept in the App’s temporary storage, which your phone clears.

Android backup. Like many apps, the App allows Android’s own backup service to back up its stories and settings to your Google account, if backup is turned on in your phone’s settings. That backup is stored by Google under your account and is governed by Google’s terms; we cannot access it. Your OpenRouter key is not included in it.

If you turn on Sync across devices, a copy of your stories and some settings is also kept in your own Google Drive, as described in section 7.

4. Writing a story (OpenRouter)

Stories are written by artificial-intelligence (AI) models reached through OpenRouter (openrouter.ai), using the OpenRouter account and key that you connect. The App only requests models that OpenRouter offers for free. When you ask for a story, the App sends OpenRouter, directly from your phone:

  • the choices for the story: the theme, the main characters’ names and traits, the listener’s age group, the companion, a loved one if you added one, the place, the quest and the story length;
  • for a returning character, the titles and short summaries of up to five of that character’s earlier stories, so the new story can feel like a continuation;
  • instructions describing how the story should be written; and
  • your OpenRouter key, which identifies your OpenRouter account.

OpenRouter forwards the request to the AI provider that runs the chosen model, and returns the story. As with any internet request, OpenRouter and the provider also receive your phone’s IP address and standard technical details of the connection. OpenRouter and the AI providers handle this information under their own privacy policies and terms, which may allow them to log it, keep it, or in the case of some free models use it to improve their services. Please review OpenRouter’s privacy policy, and do not type anything you would not want them to have. We do not receive, store or have access to these requests or the stories returned.

Connecting your account. When you connect an OpenRouter account in the App, OpenRouter’s sign-in page opens in your phone’s browser, and you sign in with OpenRouter directly. A page on this website then passes OpenRouter’s one-time code back to the App, which exchanges it with OpenRouter for your key. That page stores nothing and sends nothing to us. After connecting, and whenever you open Settings, the App also asks OpenRouter whether your key still works and how many of today’s free story requests are left; that check sends only your key.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by short “rewarded” video ads, which play when you choose to watch one (after the first few free stories, before continuing a story, and before exporting a story). The ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google.

  • Every user is treated as a child. The App tells Google that its ad requests are directed to children and come from users under the age of consent. Google then serves only non-personalized ads, does not use the information for interest-based advertising or remarketing, and limits the data it processes.
  • Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (“G”).
  • The App does not use your phone’s advertising ID; the permission that would allow it is removed from the App.
  • To deliver an ad, measure whether it played, and prevent fraud, Google’s software may still process limited technical information such as your IP address, phone model, operating-system version, app version and ad interactions. Google acts under its own policies; see Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • We do not receive personal information from Google about you, and we do not send Google your stories.

6. Reporting a story

Because stories are written by AI, the App lets you report a story that is upsetting, inappropriate or wrong (“Report a problem with this story”). Reporting is optional and only happens when you send a report. A report contains:

  • the reason you choose and any note you write;
  • the story’s title and text, and its theme, listener age group, length and whether it was interactive;
  • an internal story identifier, the date and time, the App version and the type of device system (for example, Android).

Before a report leaves your phone, the App replaces the main characters’ names and any loved one’s name with placeholders (such as “[Character 1]”), in the story and in your note. Please do not type names or other personal details in the note. Reports are received through a Google Apps Script service, stored in a private Google Sheet and emailed to our inbox, all within our Google account. We use reports only to review the story, improve the safety of the stories the App asks for, and respond to problems.

7. Other features

  • Read Aloud uses your phone’s own text-to-speech service. Most voices work on the phone itself. Some voices offered by your phone (often marked as online or network voices) may send the text being read to the voice provider, such as Google, to produce speech; this is controlled by your phone’s speech settings and that provider’s policies.
  • Bedtime reminder. If you turn it on, your phone schedules a local notification. Nothing about it is sent to us or anyone else.
  • Backup and restore. “Back up my stories” creates a file with your stories and hands it to your phone’s share sheet. Where it goes (for example, your own Google Drive) is entirely your choice and is governed by that service. We never receive or have access to it. Restoring reads a file you pick.
  • Sync across devices (optional, for adults). If you sign in with Google in Settings, the App keeps the same stories on every phone or tablet where you sign in with the same Google account. Sign-in is handled by Google on your phone. The App receives your Google account’s name and email address and a sign-in token, and keeps them only on your phone, to show which account is syncing. It asks for one Google permission, to its own app data folder in your Google Drive, which is hidden from your Drive files and cannot see or change anything else in your Drive. In that folder the App stores your stories (their text and the choices used to make them, including characters’ names), favorites, which stories you deleted, the free-story count, and these settings: story length, whether narration is on, reading speed, interactive-story preference and reminder time. It travels to Google over encrypted connections and is stored by Google under your account, subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. We never receive it and have no access to it. You can turn sync off in Settings (your stories stay on your phone), use “Delete the synced copy from Google Drive”, delete the App’s hidden data in Google Drive’s settings under Manage apps, or remove the App’s access in your Google Account’s security settings. TinyTaleZzZ’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: it is used only to sync your stories and settings for you, is never used for advertising, and is never transferred or sold to anyone.
  • Exporting and sharing. When you export a story as a storybook PDF or share it as text, it goes only where you send it. Shared text ends with a line naming the App and a link to its store page.
  • Sample stories are built into the App and involve no information about you.
  • Links. The App can open this website and OpenRouter’s website in your browser. Those sites have their own policies.

8. Phone permissions

  • Internet and network state: to write stories, load ads and send reports you choose to send.
  • Notifications, vibration and starting after a restart: only for the optional bedtime reminder, so it still arrives after the phone restarts.
  • Keeping the screen awake: while a story is open, so the screen does not turn off while you read.
  • Permissions added by Google’s ad software for delivering and measuring ads, other than the advertising-ID permission, which the App removes.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

10. How long information is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete the story, use “Clear story history”, or uninstall the App.
  • Reports: for as long as needed to review them and improve story safety, and no longer than two years, after which they are deleted.
  • In your Google Drive, if you use sync: until you delete the stories, use “Delete the synced copy from Google Drive”, or delete the App’s data from your Drive. A deleted story leaves a small note there (its identifier and when it was deleted, without its text) so your other devices delete it too.
  • Emails to us: for as long as needed to respond and keep a record of the conversation.
  • OpenRouter, AI providers and Google: according to their own policies, which we do not control.

11. Security

Information sent by the App travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your OpenRouter key is kept in your phone’s encrypted secure storage. Stories are kept in the App’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keeping your phone secured with a lock screen helps protect your stories.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Choose what to type: a first name or nickname is all a story needs.
  • Delete any story in the App, or all of them with “Clear story history” in Settings.
  • Uninstall the App to remove everything it stored on your phone.
  • Turn off the bedtime reminder, and change notification permissions in your phone’s settings.
  • Disconnect or change your OpenRouter key in the App, and manage or delete your OpenRouter account and its key at OpenRouter.
  • Turn Android backup on or off in your phone’s settings.
  • Turn Sync across devices on or off in Settings, delete the synced copy from your Google Drive, and remove the App’s access to your Google account at any time (see section 7).
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete information we hold (reports and emails), or ask questions about this policy, by writing to tinytalezzzapp@gmail.com. We will respond within the time required by law, normally within 30 days, and will not discriminate against you for using your rights. Because reports have names removed and we have no accounts, we may ask for details (such as the date and story title) to find what you are asking about.

The App is intended to be operated by adults. It does not ask children for personal information, it treats all advertising as directed to children, and it does not use persistent identifiers for interest-based advertising. A parent may contact us at any time to review or delete any information we hold about their child.

United States: state privacy laws (including California)

In the past 12 months, the only personal information we have received ourselves is the content of reports and emails people chose to send us. We do not sell or share personal information, we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information to infer characteristics, and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16. Residents of states with privacy laws may request to know, access, correct or delete personal information we hold, and may appeal a decision by writing to us. You may use an authorized agent. The App does not respond to “Do Not Track” signals because it does not track you; there is nothing to opt out of.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performing our agreement with you to provide the App (to operate its features); our legitimate interests in keeping stories safe and responding to you (reports and emails); and our legitimate interest in funding the free App with non-personalized advertising, configured as described in section 5. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because every user is treated as under the age of consent, Google does not show its consent form and serves only non-personalized ads.
